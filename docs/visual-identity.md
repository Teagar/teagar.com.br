# Visual identity — teagar.com.br

The portfolio has its **own** identity. It is a personal site for Thiago and a
storefront for Teagar Company, not another product built on the Teagar Company
design system.

Shared with the company brand: **only** the logo mark, ember orange `#FF6700`
and the typefaces (by the owner's request on 2026-11-09).

## Concept: "Golden hour"

The portrait is lit by a warm, low orange light against deep brown shadow. The
whole site is built from that light: warm darks, cream paper, one ember-orange
light source. It should read as an editorial, personal and confident site, closer
to a magazine profile than a technical spec sheet.

The signature element is **the pixel moon**: an original orange pixel-art moon
(chunky pixels, an 8-colour stepped palette from `#FFD2A1` to `#3D1702`, a 1 px
outline, hand-placed-looking craters, a Bayer-dithered terminator) rising behind
the portrait in a night sky. **The stage follows the theme:** dark theme shows the
night with the moon; light theme shows a day with an original **pixel sun** (round
disc, a warm 1 px rim instead of the moon's dark outline, a bright core with stepped
orange bands Bayer-dithered at each edge, no craters), eight chunky rays in two frames (`sun-rays-1/2.svg`, the second turned
half a step so alternating them shimmers), a warm banded day sky (`day-sky.svg`,
`#FFD9B0` to `#FFF1E0` with three ground/haze bands, never a generic blue) and four
hand-drawn pixel clouds (white/cream body, warm underside, inline SVG, groups
`c1`/`c2`) placed in open sky beside the portrait.
The sun is generated by `scripts/sun.mjs` (same helpers, seeded, deterministic).
Both scenes are in the markup and CSS picks one from `html[data-theme]` (no-JS
falls back to `prefers-color-scheme`). The moon is generated by code
(`scripts/moon.mjs`, seeded and deterministic) into `moon.svg`, `moon-halo.svg`,
`moon-stars.svg` and `moon-sky.svg`, all `crispEdges`. One logical pixel is a whole
number of CSS pixels (`--px-size` 3 to 6 by breakpoint). The small ember dot still
serves as the section marker and the card glow.

**Pixel tea:** "Teagar" puns on tea, so a second, smaller pixel-art piece sits in
the contact section and the footer: an original orange teacup on a saucer with a
tea-bag tag (hinting the T), in the moon's palette plus only `#FFF1E0` and `#2A1D14`.
`scripts/tea.mjs` (reusing the moon helpers) generates `tea-cup.svg` (48x40),
`tea-glint.svg` and three `tea-steam-N.svg` frames (48x24), all `crispEdges`, seeded
and deterministic.

## Do NOT inherit from the Teagar Company design system

- No uppercase headlines, no italic emphasis, no letter-spaced eyebrows.
- No blueprint grid, crop marks, registration ticks, coordinates, "FIG. 01" captions.
- No blueprint blue `#1F4B8C`, charcoal `#2B2A28`, paper `#F0F1F3`.
- No 2–4 px "almost square" radii everywhere; no hairline-boxed card grids.
- No "01 — Section" kicker split into number + point mark.

Measured: text/bg 16:1 in both themes; muted ≥ 5.5:1; `--ember-text` ≥ 4.5:1 on
`--bg` and `--surface-2` (verify with the contrast test). `#FF6700` on cream is
2.5:1, so use it on light backgrounds only for shapes or text ≥ 32 px bold.

No gradients except one: a soft radial ember glow (`radial-gradient` of
`--ember` at ≤ 18% opacity to transparent) used behind the card hover glow. No glassmorphism, no blur.

## Typography

- **Typography (design-system fonts, owner request 2026-11-09):** the owner
  asked for the Teagar design-system typefaces, used with these roles only:
  - **Display** (`--display`): *Big Shoulders Display*, variable 100–900.
    Headings (h1, h2, h3), stat numbers, section numerals and the pull
    quote. Headings use weight 800, sentence case, tracking -0.005em and
    leading 0.95–1.0. The hero emphasis (`titleB`) is the same face at
    weight 300 colored `--ember`; there is no italic anywhere.
  - **Body and UI** (`--body`): *Public Sans*, variable 100–900. Body 400,
    buttons and nav 600, sentence case, body 17–18 px, line-height 1.6.
  - **Small labels** (`--mono`): *IBM Plex Mono* 400/500/600 for card
    category and year, status chips, stack chips, fact labels, form labels,
    footer meta and marquee group labels. Weight 500,
    12–13 px, tracking .02em, sentence case, `--muted`.
- Self-host woff2 (latin + latin-ext) under `public/fonts/` with the OFL text.

Scale (clamp): hero name 64→129 px; h2 51→101 px; h3 24→32 px; stat numbers and section numerals scaled about 15% up from the earlier scale.

## Shape and space

- Radii: 999 px for buttons, chips and the language/theme controls; 24 px for
  cards and images; the hero portrait stage has no radius (its edges fade into the page, see "Hero stage fade").
- Generous whitespace: sections 120–176 px vertical on desktop, 80 px mobile.
- 12-column fluid layout, max content width 1240 px, gutters 24–32 px.
- Borders are rare; separate with surface color changes and space.
- Shadows: none in dark; in light one soft warm shadow for raised cards
  (`0 1px 0 rgba(26,19,14,.04), 0 12px 32px -12px rgba(120,60,10,.18)`).

## Motion

Calm and physical. Durations 500–900 ms, `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)`.

- Hero load: headline lines rise out of a mask (stagger 80 ms); the moon rises
  from below the stage with an ease-out over 1.3 s, then its three
  halo rings fade in one by one (0.15 s apart); stars fade in by group; cut-out
  portrait rises 32 px.
- Hero idle (steps only, never smooth): the moon bobs 1 logical pixel every 3 s;
  the brightest halo ring cycles every 1.2 s; star groups twinkle at 1.6/2.3/3.1 s;
  a plus-sparkle blinks bigger about every 7 s.
- Hero pointer (fine pointers only): the moon shifts up to 3 logical pixels
  toward the pointer, stars 1 px opposite, portrait 6 px opposite; moon and star
  offsets are snapped to the logical pixel grid in JS.
- Day scene (light theme, steps only): the sun rises like the moon and bobs 1 px;
  the two ray frames alternate every 0.6 s; cloud group c1 drifts 10 logical px and
  c2 7 px, one logical pixel per step, ping-pong over 8 s and 5.6 s.
- Theme swap (0.7 s total, eased, owner request): toggling the theme while the hero is
  visible cross-fades the new pixel sky over the old one with ease-in-out, so the stage
  passes through dusk or dawn tones; the current body sets with ease-in (0.35 s) and the
  other rises with ease-out (0.5 s, starting at 0.2 s). The portrait stays in front.
  Large moves (load rise, swap) are eased; small idle loops (bob, twinkle, rays, ring
  pulse, clouds) stay in pixel steps. Reduced motion: instant swap.
- Scroll ("moonset"; the sun sets the same way): the moon sinks up to 8 logical pixels, snapped to the grid,
  while the halo fades out in 4 steps.
- Cards: a 280 px radial ember glow follows the pointer inside the card on hover.
- Stack: one slow horizontal marquee row of technologies (pauses on hover and
  focus).
- Section headings and rows fade-rise 16 px on first view.
- Pixel tea (steps only): steam cycles its three frames every 1.05 s; the tea
  glint blinks about every 4 s; the contact cup pops in (scale snapped in 4 steps)
  when the section enters the viewport. Without JS or under reduced motion it is a
  static cup with steam frame 1.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables every animation and transform (moon,
  halo and stars render static in their final positions), the marquee becomes a
  wrapped list, and content is visible without JS.

## Accessibility floor (unchanged)

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in both themes, visible focus (3 px `--ember` ring with
2 px offset, `--ember-text` ring on light surfaces if needed for 3:1), 44 px
targets, semantic landmarks, one h1, progressive enhancement, no horizontal
scroll from 320 px.


## Pixel icons

The work section reuses the moon's pixel-art language: 24x24 project icons
(one per `work.items[].icon`) and 8x8 status icons, generated at build by
`scripts/pixel-icons.mjs` from hand-placed ASCII maps (char to colour legend)
into `dist/assets/img/icons/`. Palette: the moon's `PAL` ramp with a 1 px
`#3D1702` outline and light from the upper right, plus only cream `#FFF1E0`,
dark brown `#2A1D14`, grey-stone `#A89C8E` (neutral accents) and green
`#2E9E62` (reserved for the "live" status). Status icons are decorative
(`aria-hidden`), drawn at 2 CSS px per pixel and animated with `steps()`
sprites: hourglass (in development, 3 frames), blinking broadcast dot (live),
`</>` (open source). All designs are original; no third-party marks.

## Theme switch timing (owner request 2026-11-12)

- When the theme toggle is pressed (and reduced motion is off), the whole page's colors (background, text, borders, fills, shadows) transition with the same 0.7 s `cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)` as the moon/sun sky cross-fade, so page and sky change together. The toggle sets `html.theme-anim` for 1.5 s; inherited colors trail their parent slightly, so the class outlives the 0.7 s to avoid a snap. The hero stage keeps its own motion and is excluded.

## Hero stage fade (owner request 2026-11-13)

The face and the sun/moon stay fully opaque; the theme swap animation is unchanged.

## Moon easter egg (owner request 2026-11-13)

- Three quick clicks or taps on the moon or the sun (each within 0.6 s of the last; sun added 2026-11-13) toggle `.egg` on the stage: the pixel moon or sun fades out and the Teagar Company logo pops in at the same spot, used as a **mask over the body's own pixel texture** (`mask: url(/assets/brand/logo.svg)`), so the whole logo is made of moon or sun. The texture is enlarged so it fills the logo (moon 165%; sun 160% shifted to 70% so its pale centre falls in the C's counter, with `#FF7A1F` filling the tips past the disc), and the logo sits slightly lower (86%, 12% from the top) to stay clear of the stage's top fade; three more bring it back. The state is shared, so switching theme keeps the logo in the other sky. Not announced or hinted anywhere (it is an easter egg), decorative (`aria-hidden`), not persisted. The stage disables text selection so a triple click does not select the paragraph, and both bodies use `touch-action: manipulation` so taps do not zoom. Reduced motion makes the swap instant.


## Easter eggs and the secret /cheats/ page

Seven more easter eggs join the moon/sun logo. All of them are listed on a secret page, `/cheats/` (pt-BR) and `/en/cheats/` (en). That page is `noindex, nofollow`, has no canonical, is left out of the sitemap and llms.txt, and no page links to it. The only hint is the console note. None of the eggs changes anything until it is triggered, and each one ends by itself.

| Egg | Trigger | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Tea time | visitor's clock between 16:00 and 17:00 | the contact line becomes "Já é hora do chá…" (a `<template>` per page) and the cup steams faster |
| Full cup | 5 clicks on the footer cup within 4 s | a pixel drip per click; on the fifth, tea domes over the rim, runs down both sides, pools on the saucer, and a toast says "Teagar = tea + gar" |
| Konami | ↑↑↓↓←→←→BA | `html.gameboy` for 10 s: an SVG filter (`#gb-filter`) maps the page to the 4 Game Boy greens; the filter goes on `.topbar`, `main` and `.footer`, never on `body`, so the fixed bar keeps working |
| Console | opening DevTools | the TC logo in ASCII (`#FF6700`), a one-line pitch, the email and a hint to `/cheats/` |
| Shooting star | 20 s without input while the night hero is on screen | a pixel streak crosses the sky; clicking it while it flies pauses it and shows the wish toast |
| Typing teagar | typing t-e-a-g-a-r outside form fields | the header name rolls to Thiago (the same state as hover) and 16 pixel teacups rain for about 2 s |
| Eclipse | 5 theme switches, each within 1.5 s of the last | once the last sky swap ends, the scene dims, a black moon silhouette crosses the sun, and a pale corona glows at totality (3.4 s) |

Toasts use one `#eggToast` (`role="status"`) in `--text` on `--bg`. Under reduced motion there is no drip, rain or star; the spill appears at its final size, and the eclipse holds totality instead of moving.
